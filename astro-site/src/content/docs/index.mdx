<section class="home-band home-band--muted">
<span class="eyebrow">Find the bottleneck</span>
<h2>Understand the cost of inference.</h2>
<p>Measure the model as a whole, then investigate the operations and hardware activity behind the result.</p>
<div class="feature-strip feature-mosaic">
<article><Icon class="feature-icon" name="stopwatch" /><h3>Measure the model. Locate the cost.</h3><p>Start with latency from a clean inference window, then inspect a separate instrumented pass to find the operators worth optimizing.</p><div class="timing-result"><span>heartKIT AFIB · heliaAOT</span><strong>12.14 <small>ms</small></strong><span>Median latency · Apollo510 @ 96 MHz</span></div><ol class="timing-workflow"><li><strong>Establish the baseline</strong><span>Measure the complete inference without per-layer counter reads. Keep the model, clock and measurement settings with the result.</span></li><li><strong>Rank the expensive operators</strong><span>Inspect cycles and each layer’s share of the instrumented total. Start with the layers that account for the most work.</span></li><li><strong>Test a targeted improvement</strong><span>Try another engine or memory placement, then compare the saved runs to see whether whole-model latency improved.</span></li></ol><a href="/helia-profiler/guide/latency/">Understand timing and measurement boundaries →</a></article>
<article><Icon class="feature-icon" name="microchip" /><h3>Investigate costly layers</h3><p>Look beyond cycle counts. On supported targets, inspect memory accesses, processor stalls and MVE activity alongside each operator.</p><p class="feature-detail">Use the counters to investigate whether memory placement or kernel execution deserves a closer look. Available counters depend on the target.</p><a href="/helia-profiler/guide/pmu-counters/">Explore counters →</a></article>
<article><Icon class="feature-icon" name="bolt" /><h3>Include power and energy</h3><p>Capture the monitored supply with a supported Joulescope or INA228. Read power during the measurement window and energy per inference.</p><p class="feature-detail">Save the capture with the run, inspect its validity and compare energy alongside latency. Requires a configured power monitor.</p><a href="/helia-profiler/guide/power/">Set up power capture →</a></article>
</div>
</section>

<section class="home-band" id="saved-results">
<span class="eyebrow">Compare your options</span>
<h2>Put your next idea to the test.</h2>
<p>Compare timing, memory and energy alongside the settings that produced them. hpx flags incompatible measurements and leaves out per-layer differences when operation sequences do not match.</p>
<div class="feature-rows">
<article><h3>Evaluate engines and compilers</h3><p>Keep the model and target fixed, then measure the effect of an engine or compiler change.</p><a href="/helia-profiler/examples/engine-comparison/">Compare engines →</a></article>
<article><h3>Explore memory placement</h3><p>Try supported placements for weights and working memory, then measure the effect on your target.</p><a href="/helia-profiler/guide/memory/">Plan memory →</a></article>
<article><h3>Inspect the model graph</h3><p>View captured layer measurements on the model graph. Use host-side model analysis to inspect operations and tensor sizes before connecting a board.</p><a href="/helia-profiler/guide/model-explorer/">Explore the graph →</a></article>
</div>
</section>
