## From model to results in three steps

Choose a model and board. heliaPROFILER builds the test firmware, loads it
onto your board, and saves the measurements for you.

    can repeat the experiment.
  </LinkCard>
  <LinkCard href="/helia-profiler/getting-started/validate-your-setup/" title="2. Run on your board" tone="filled">

### Choose the hardware and toolchain for your run

Start with Apollo510 EVB or Apollo3P EVB, the **2 stable boards** among
**13 boards**. Other boards, including the Atomiq110 FPGA carrier, have
preview support. Choose from **3 toolchains**: GNU Arm Embedded, Arm Compiler 6,
or Arm Toolchain for Embedded (preview).
See the [board guide](/helia-profiler/guide/boards/),
[compiler setup](/helia-profiler/guide/toolchains/) and
[tested combinations](/helia-profiler/reference/compatibility/)
to choose a setup for your model.
