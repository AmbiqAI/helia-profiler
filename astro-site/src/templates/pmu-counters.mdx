## Purpose

Choose what the per-layer measurement counts. hpx captures cycles,
instructions, cache and bus activity, and on a Cortex-M55 the MVE vector
unit and, on Atomiq110, the Ethos-U NPU. The tables on this page are
generated from the counter registry of the hpx you are reading about, so
the names and groups are the ones the profiler accepts.

## Prerequisites

A working profile ([First profile](../../getting-started/first-profile/))
and a board whose family you know from [Boards and targets](../boards/).

## Which groups your board has

{/* PMU_CATALOG:socs */}

The rows come from `list_socs()` and `SocDef.profiling_domains` through
`supported_groups_for_domains()` in `src/helia_profiler/platform/`. On a
DWT-only SoC the profiler warns and captures cycle counts only; a
`pmu_counters` selection has no effect there, and the `mve` group is
rejected at preflight.

## Steps

### 1. Select counters

`profiling.pmu_counters` maps a group to a selection:

| Selection | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `default` | the curated subset for that group, sized to fit one pass |
| `all` | every counter in the group, captured over several passes |
| `[name, ...]` | an explicit list of counter names |

```yaml
profiling:
  pmu_counters:
    cpu: default
    memory: [ARM_PMU_L1D_CACHE, ARM_PMU_L1D_CACHE_MISS_RD, ARM_PMU_DTCM_ACCESS]
    mve: all
```

```bash
hpx profile model.tflite --pmu-counters cpu:default --pmu-counters mve:all
hpx profile model.tflite --pmu-counters mve:ARM_PMU_MVE_INST_RETIRED,ARM_PMU_MVE_STALL
```

`per_layer` defaults to `true`, `iterations` to `100`, `warmup` to `5` and
`aggregation` to `median`. Names are case-sensitive and must match the
catalogue exactly; an unknown name fails with the full sorted list.

The Ethos-U counters are sampled from the NPU's own PMU through the core
driver, per command stream, and only mean something for Vela-compiled
models on the `ethos_u` engine backend; layers the NPU never dispatched
are reported through the `NPU_DISPATCHED` column rather than as zeros.

### 3. Multi-pass capture

<span id="multi-pass-profiling"></span>

The Cortex-M55 PMU has 8 hardware counter slots; hpx chains pairs into
32-bit logical counters to avoid overflow, so each firmware pass captures
4 counters per group. A larger selection is planned into passes, each pass
builds, flashes and captures `warmup + iterations` inferences, and the
results merge into one per-layer table. `profile_results.csv` is always
the merged result; `output.detailed: true` adds `detailed/profile_<group>.csv`
and `profile_<group>_<pass>.csv`. Selecting `all` on every group spans
the whole catalogue and runs many independent passes; use `default` or an
explicit list while iterating.

### 4. Aggregation and iterations

`profiling.iterations` inferences per pass, after `profiling.warmup`
discarded runs, combined per layer by `profiling.aggregation`:

| Method | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| `median` (default) | robust to corrupted or outlier iterations |
| `mean` | arithmetic average |
| `trimmed` | drops the high and low extremes, then averages |

All three first reject structurally invalid samples (a uint32 wrap, a
frozen-zero row) and log how many; a counter with no surviving samples is
omitted rather than replaced. `profiling.per_layer: false` captures
whole-model counters only. Every run also performs a clean end-to-end
pass with no per-layer instrumentation and reports its cycle count next
to the per-layer sum; a small delta between the two is the
instrumentation overhead and is shown in the terminal.

### 5. Read the results

- The terminal's top-layers table: layers by cycles with their share of
  the total, and on a Cortex-M55 the MVE instruction share and MACs per
  MVE instruction.
- `profile_results.csv`: one row per layer, one column per captured
  counter, merged across passes; `overflow` is `True` for a layer whose
  counter saturated, whose values are then a lower bound.
- `detailed/profile_<group>.csv` and per-pass files with `output.detailed`.
- `model_explorer/me_overlay_<COUNTER>.json`, one per counter, for
  [Model Explorer](../model-explorer/).

| Derived metric | Formula | Where |
|---|---|---|
| L1D hit rate | `(1 - L1D_CACHE_MISS_RD / L1D_CACHE_RD) x 100%`, falling back to `L1D_CACHE` and `L1D_CACHE_REFILL` as a complete pair | `summary.json`, `detailed/memory.json` |
| MVE instruction share | `MVE_INST_RETIRED / INST_RETIRED x 100%` | terminal |
| MVE MAC density | `MVE_INT_MAC_RETIRED / MVE_INST_RETIRED` | terminal |
| MVE load/store density | `MVE_LDST_RETIRED / MVE_INST_RETIRED` | terminal |
| MVE stall share | `MVE_STALL / CPU_CYCLES x 100%` | terminal |

A derived rate is omitted, not guessed, when no consistent counter pair
covers every layer.

### 6. Apollo3 and Apollo4: the debugger stays attached

On the Cortex-M4F families the `DWT->CYCCNT` cycle counter lives in the
core debug power domain, powered only while a debugger asserts the Debug
Access Port's `CDBGPWRUPREQ`, which firmware cannot set. If the host
released the probe after reset the domain would power down mid-run and
every per-layer cycle count would read 0. The `rtt` and `swo` transports
hold a debugger attached anyway; for `uart` and `usb_cdc` hpx detects
these families and keeps a `pylink` session attached for the whole
capture. Apollo5 boards keep releasing the probe after reset: their
Armv8-M PMU does not need it and the secure bootloader prefers it. The
dedicated power binary never has a debugger, which is why it times its
window with STIMER on those families (`SocCapabilities.power_window_timer`).

Each SoC also reserves a fixed number of per-operator PMU records
(`SocDef.pmu_max_ops`); a model with more operators than the reservation
does not fit, and the limit is per SoC rather than a configuration key.

## Expected output

`profile_results.csv` with one column per requested counter,
`overflow_detected: false` in `summary.json`, and a clean-pass cycle count
close to the per-layer sum.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `overflow_detected: true` | a counter saturated its 32-bit accumulator on a very long or hot layer | fewer iterations, or fewer or narrower counters for that layer |
| selections silently ignored | a DWT-only SoC | expected; `hpx profile --verbose` shows the warning |
| per-layer cycles read 0 on Apollo4 | the probe was released early over `uart` or `usb_cdc` | check for a custom reset or lifecycle override |
| `all` takes a long time | many independent build, flash and capture passes | narrow the selection |
| unknown counter name | case or spelling | copy it from the catalogue above |
| group not supported for this target | `mve` needs a Cortex-M55; `ethos_npu` needs Atomiq110 | pick a group the family table lists |
